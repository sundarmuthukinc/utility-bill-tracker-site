Privacy Policy

Utility Bill Tracker is a personal tool used only by its owner. Last updated: November 15, 2026.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google Drive and Google Sheets to upload the owner's own utility bill PDFs to the owner's Drive folders and to add bill details (date, description, amount) to the owner's spreadsheets.

How data is used

Data is used only to keep the owner's bill records up to date. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google user data is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Storage

Login tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Bill files stay in the owner's Google Drive.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
